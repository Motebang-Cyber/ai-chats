AI  Chats

Radio Meets Ai

Ai Chats is a personalized Ai-powered radio platform that combines conversational Ai, Current affairs,
job opportunities, music discovery, and personalized content into one listening experience.

Instead of simply displaying lists of jobs, news articles, or music recommendations, Ai Chats presents
personalized information through an AI radio experience where virtual hosts discuss and present content
specifically for each listener.

Core Idea
You talk. Ai listens. Your radio adapts.

Ai Chats learns from the user's profile, skills, career interests, listening behaviour, saved jobs, skipped content,
preffered topics, and other authorized information to create a more personalized experience.

Main Content
AI Chats will combine four major content areas:

1)AI Radio
*Ai-generated radio-style conversations
*Multiple AI hosts.
*Personalized discussions
*Spoken Audio using text-to-speech

2)Job Posts
*Discover relevant job opportunities
*Match jobs against the user's skills and career interests.
*Present suitable opportunities through the Ai radio experience.
*Allow users to save and interact with jobs.

3)Music
*Personalized music recommedations
*Music preferences and listening behaviour
*integration with authorized music providers

4)Current Affairs
*Personalized news and current-affairs topics
*Source attribution
*AI-generated discussion and summaries
*Seperation between reported facts and Ai-generated commentary

Personalization
Each user should receive a different experience based on information such as:
*skills
*cv Information
*Career level
*Job interests
*Location preferences
*Current-affairs
*Music preferences
*Listening behaviour
*Jobs viewed
*Jobs skipped
*Jobs Applied for
*News topics listened to or skipped
*Music listening behaviour
